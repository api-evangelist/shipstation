Your customer just checked out—and then your marketing goes quiet right when they're checking their inbox the most. ShipStation's new Klaviyo integration changes that by feeding real-time shipment tracking data directly into your marketing platform. The result: automated, personalized updates for every key delivery milestone that reduce WISMO support tickets, boost customer LTV, and give marketers high open-rate touchpoints they didn't have before—all built on data already sitting in ShipStation.
