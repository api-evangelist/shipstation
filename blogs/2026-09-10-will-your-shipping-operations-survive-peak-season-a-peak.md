For ecommerce sellers in Europe, peak season can mean months of overlapping demand, cross-border complexity, carrier capacity constraints, and a second wave of returns. This checklist covers the key steps to prepare your shipping and fulfillment operation, from building a flexible carrier strategy and testing cross-border compliance to automating repetitive processes, syncing inventory, managing returns, and more. Use it to identify operational gaps before peak volume exposes them.
